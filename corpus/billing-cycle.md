# Billing cycle and invoices

Your billing date is the day of the month you first subscribed. If you
subscribed on the 15th, you are charged on the 15th of every month (or
once a year on that date for annual billing). If your billing date falls
on the 29th, 30th, or 31st and a month does not have that day, you are
charged on the last day of that month.

When you add users in the middle of a cycle, the extra seats are charged
on a **pro-rated** basis on your next invoice — you pay only for the
remaining part of the cycle. When you remove users, the seats stay active
until the end of the current cycle and are not charged again.

Invoices are available under **Settings → Billing → Invoices**. Each
invoice can be downloaded as a PDF. TaskNest does not email invoices
automatically; they are only available in the app.

If a payment fails, we retry the card after 3 days and again after 7
days. If the second retry fails, the workspace is paused (read-only)
until payment succeeds.
