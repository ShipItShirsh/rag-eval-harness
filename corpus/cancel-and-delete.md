# Cancelling and deleting your account

**Cancelling** a paid plan is done by the workspace owner from
**Settings → Billing → Cancel plan**. The workspace stays on the paid
plan until the end of the current billing period, then moves to the free
Starter plan. Cancellation does not delete any data.

**Deleting the workspace** is permanent. Only the owner can do it, from
**Settings → Danger zone → Delete workspace**. You must type the
workspace name to confirm. Deletion takes effect immediately and cannot
be undone: all projects, tasks, comments, and files are permanently
removed. There is no recovery window and no backup restore after a
workspace is deleted, so export your data first if you need it.

Deleting your **personal account** (leaving all workspaces) is under
**Settings → Profile → Delete my account**. You must first transfer
ownership of, or leave, every workspace you belong to.
