# Changing your plan

You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from **Settings → Billing →
Change plan**. Only workspace owners and billing admins can change the
plan.

**Upgrades take effect immediately.** If you upgrade in the middle of a
billing cycle, you are charged the difference between the two plans for
the remaining part of the cycle, on your next invoice.

**Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle.** Your
current plan — and its limits and features — stays in place until then.
No refund is issued for the difference.

If you downgrade to a plan with lower limits than you currently use (for
example, more projects than the Starter plan allows), your existing data
is **not** deleted. The workspace becomes read-only for the items over
the limit until you remove them or upgrade again.
