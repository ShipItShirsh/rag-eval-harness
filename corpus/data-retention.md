# Data retention and backups

TaskNest keeps your workspace data for as long as your workspace exists.
If a workspace is downgraded to the free Starter plan, data over the
plan's limits is kept read-only, not deleted.

TaskNest backs up all workspace data **every 24 hours** and keeps backups
for **30 days**. Backups exist to recover from system failures on our
side. Support cannot restore a single workspace, project, or task from a
backup at a customer's request — deleted items can only be recovered from
the in-app trash, which keeps deleted tasks for 30 days.

Data is stored in data centres in the **United States (Virginia)**. For
Business-plan workspaces, data at rest is encrypted, and all connections
use TLS encryption in transit.
