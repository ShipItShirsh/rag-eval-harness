# Exporting your data

Any member can export a single project's tasks to **CSV** from the
project menu (**… → Export**). The CSV includes task titles, assignees,
statuses, due dates, and comments.

Workspace owners and admins can export the **entire workspace** —
projects, tasks, comments, and member list — as **CSV or JSON** from
**Settings → Data → Export workspace**. File attachments are not included
in workspace exports; they must be downloaded individually.

A workspace export is prepared in the background. When it is ready (usually
within **15 minutes** for most workspaces), the requester gets an email
with a download link. The link expires after **48 hours**.

There is no charge for exporting your data, on any plan, including after
cancellation while the workspace is still in its paid period.
