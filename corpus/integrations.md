# Integrations

Integrations are available on the **Pro and Business plans**. TaskNest
integrates with:

- **Slack** — get task notifications in a channel and create tasks from
  messages.
- **Google Calendar** — task due dates appear on a calendar you choose.
- **GitHub** — link pull requests to tasks; closing the pull request can
  mark the task Done.
- **Zapier** — connect TaskNest to other apps through Zapier triggers and
  actions.

To connect an integration, go to **Settings → Integrations**, choose the
service, and sign in to authorize it. Each integration is connected per
workspace, not per user.

TaskNest does **not** have integrations with Microsoft Teams, Jira, or
Salesforce. There is no way to build a custom integration except through
Zapier or the API.
