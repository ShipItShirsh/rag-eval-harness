# Notification settings

TaskNest notifies you when you are assigned a task, mentioned in a
comment, or when a task you watch changes status or passes its due date.

You can change what you are notified about under **Settings →
Notifications**. Email notifications and in-app notifications are
controlled separately. Push notifications on mobile follow the in-app
settings.

To pause notifications, turn on **Pause notifications** in the same
settings page, or set **Do not disturb** hours — during those hours,
notifications are held and delivered when the period ends.

Notification settings are **per user**, not per workspace: an admin
cannot change another member's notification settings, and there is no
workspace-wide mute.
