# Resetting your password

If you have forgotten your password:

1. Go to the sign-in page and choose **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email address on your account.
3. Open the reset email and choose **Reset password**. The link expires
   after **1 hour**.
4. Enter a new password. Passwords must be at least **12 characters** and
   include at least one number.

Changing your password signs you out of TaskNest on all other devices.

If you do not receive the reset email within a few minutes, check your
spam folder. If you signed up with single sign-on (SSO), you do not have
a TaskNest password — sign in with your identity provider instead, and
change your password there.

TaskNest support can never see your password and cannot reset it for you
manually.
