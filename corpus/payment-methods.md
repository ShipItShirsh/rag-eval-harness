# Payment methods

TaskNest accepts the following payment methods for paid plans:

- Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover cards
- Debit cards with a Visa or Mastercard logo

We do **not** accept PayPal, bank transfers (ACH or wire), checks, or
cryptocurrency.

To update your card, go to **Settings → Billing → Payment method**. The
new card is used from the next charge onward; past charges are never
moved to a new card. Only workspace owners and billing admins can change
the payment method.

All card payments are processed over an encrypted connection. TaskNest
never sees or stores your full card number.
