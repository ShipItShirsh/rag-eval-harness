# Plans and pricing

TaskNest has three plans. Prices are per user, per month, billed in US
dollars.

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing (per month) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | Free | Free |
| Pro | $12 | $10 |
| Business | $24 | $20 |

**Starter** includes up to 3 projects, 5 users, and 100 MB of file storage.
**Pro** includes unlimited projects, up to 50 users, 10 GB of file storage,
and integrations. **Business** includes unlimited users, 100 GB of file
storage, single sign-on (SSO), and priority support with a 4-hour response
target.

Annual billing is charged once a year up front. You can switch between
monthly and annual billing at any time from **Settings → Billing**; the
change takes effect at the next billing date.
