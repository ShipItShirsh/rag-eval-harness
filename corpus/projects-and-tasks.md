# Projects and tasks

A **project** is a collection of related tasks. A **task** has a title, an
optional description, one assignee, an optional due date, and a status:
**To do**, **In progress**, or **Done**.

To create a task, open the project and choose **+ Add task**. To assign
it, choose the assignee from the people picker — a task can have only one
assignee, but you can add watchers who get notified about changes.

Tasks can have **subtasks** (up to 5 levels deep) and **attachments** of
up to **25 MB** per file. You can also add comments; typing **@** followed
by a name mentions a member and notifies them.

Deleting a task moves it to the project's trash for **30 days**, after
which it is permanently deleted. Owners and admins can restore tasks from
the trash during those 30 days.

The Starter plan allows up to 3 projects. Pro and Business plans allow
unlimited projects.
