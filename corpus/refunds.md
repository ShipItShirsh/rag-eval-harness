# Refunds

TaskNest offers a **30-day money-back guarantee** on the first payment for
a new paid plan. If you cancel within 30 days of your first charge, you
can request a full refund of that charge — no questions asked, and no
partial-month calculation.

After the first 30 days, payments are **non-refundable**. This includes
annual plans cancelled part-way through the year: the plan stays active
until the end of the paid period, but no refund is issued for the unused
time.

Refunds for duplicate charges or billing errors are always issued in
full, at any time.

To request a refund, go to **Settings → Billing → Request a refund** and
choose the charge. Refunds are issued to the original payment method and
take **5–10 business days** to appear, depending on your bank. TaskNest
cannot refund to a different card or by bank transfer.
