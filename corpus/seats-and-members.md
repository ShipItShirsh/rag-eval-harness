# Seats and members

On paid plans, you are charged **per seat**. Every member of the
workspace — including the owner — uses one seat. Guests are not available
on TaskNest; anyone who needs access needs a seat.

To invite a member, go to **Settings → Members → Invite** and enter their
email address. Invitations expire after **7 days**; you can resend an
invitation from the same page. An invited person does not use a seat
until they accept the invitation.

To remove a member, go to **Settings → Members**, choose the member, and
select **Remove**. Their tasks are not deleted — they become unassigned,
and the workspace owner can reassign them. A removed member loses access
immediately. Their seat stays paid until the end of the current billing
cycle and is then not renewed.

There are three roles: **Owner** (one per workspace; full control,
including billing and deletion), **Admin** (manages members, projects,
and settings, but cannot delete the workspace), and **Member** (works on
projects and tasks).
