# Contacting support and response times

You can contact TaskNest support from inside the app (**Help → Contact
support**) or by email at **support@tasknest.example**. Support is
available in English only.

Response targets (first reply from a human):

- **Starter** — within 2 business days
- **Pro** — within 1 business day
- **Business** — within 4 hours, at any time (24/7)

TaskNest does not offer phone support on any plan, and there is no live
chat. Support hours for Starter and Pro are Monday to Friday, 9:00–17:00
US Eastern Time, excluding US public holidays.

Support can help with billing, account access, and product questions.
Support cannot change another member's settings, unlock a locked account
early, or restore deleted workspaces.
