# Troubleshooting sign-in problems

If you cannot sign in, work through these checks in order:

1. **Caps lock and email spelling.** Sign-in is not case-sensitive for
   the email address, but passwords are case-sensitive.
2. **SSO workspaces.** If your workspace uses single sign-on, the email
   and password form will not work for you — use the **Sign in with SSO**
   button instead.
3. **Locked account.** After **10 failed sign-in attempts**, an account
   is locked for **30 minutes**. Waiting is the only way to unlock it;
   support cannot unlock an account early.
4. **Two-factor problems.** If you lost your authenticator app, use one
   of your recovery codes. If you have no recovery codes, contact support
   — identity verification takes up to 3 business days.
5. **Browser problems.** TaskNest requires cookies and JavaScript. Try a
   private/incognito window; if sign-in works there, clear the site's
   cookies in your normal browser.
