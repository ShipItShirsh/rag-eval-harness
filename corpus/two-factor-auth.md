# Two-factor authentication

TaskNest supports two-factor authentication (2FA) with an authenticator
app (such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, or 1Password).
TaskNest does **not** support 2FA by SMS or email.

To turn on 2FA:

1. Go to **Settings → Security → Two-factor authentication**.
2. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app.
3. Enter the 6-digit code from the app to confirm.

When you enable 2FA, TaskNest shows you **10 recovery codes**. Save them
somewhere safe — each code works once, and they are the only way back in
if you lose your authenticator app. If you lose both your app and your
recovery codes, you must contact support and verify your identity; this
process takes up to **3 business days**.

Workspace admins on the Business plan can require 2FA for all members
from **Settings → Security**.
